The Art of Implementation

I believe creativity is only as powerful as one's ability to execute it. My work began with physical materials, from print-making to resin sculpture. Making art taught me to prototype, experiment, and iterate. The medium changed, but my curiosity didn't.

Software engineering expanded the possibilities of what I could build. Understanding the constraints of technology has made me a better designer. Solving the problems that stand between an idea and its implementation is where real innovation begins. Bringing these two worlds together has shaped how I approach technology beyond traditional screens.

Prinsys

Early Leakage Detection

A battery-free sensor system designed to detect and monitor water leaks across commercial properties. Our international team developed the product end to end, spanning research, service design, UX/UI, system architecture, hardware integration, and development. We delivered a working application and receiver prototype to Prinsys for continued development.

Encrypted Chat

Secure Instant Messaging

A full-stack, real-time application built from the ground up using Flask, Socket.IO, and JavaScript, with client-side AES-256 encryption that keeps plaintext inaccessible to the server. I engineered the messaging architecture, presence tracking, state synchronization, and connection recovery across multiple clients, then validated the system’s performance and resilience through packet-level network analysis.

Algorithmic Crafting

Human Centered Design

A multi-year research study exploring how knots can be learned through a rule-based visual language. The work evolved from paper prototypes to digital puzzle interfaces and immersive XR experiments, combining prototyping with iterative user testing. I helped develop and evaluate systems that used modular rope segments to study shape grammar, spatial reasoning and computational thinking.

Islanding NYC

3D Animation

A visualization of New York sinking beneath water, created in response to the Lower Manhattan Coastal Resiliency Project and growing flood risk. Using Blender, I built the water simulation and a custom shader with animated caustics that recreate light movement through water. The final 90-second animation was rendered and composited in After Effects for an event hosted by the Mellon Foundation.
