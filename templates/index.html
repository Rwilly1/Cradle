The Art of Implementation

I believe creativity is only as powerful as one's ability to execute it. My work began with physical materials, from print-making to resin sculpture. Making art taught me to prototype, experiment, and iterate. The medium changed, but my curiosity didn't.

Software engineering expanded the possibilities of what I could build. Understanding the constraints of technology has made me a better designer. Solving the problems that stand between an idea and its implementation is where real innovation begins. Bringing these two worlds together has shaped how I approach technology beyond traditional screens.
